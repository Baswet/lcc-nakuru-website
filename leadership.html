Lead Pastor

Rev. Elijah Mwangi

Rev. Elijah Mwangi is the Lead Pastor at Life Celebration Centre of Deliverance Church International, Nakuru. With a deep passion for planting and strengthening churches, he has raised and equipped leaders locally and internationally through preaching, teaching and intentional discipleship.

He holds a Master’s Degree in Theology from Beacon University College of Ministry (USA), a Master’s Degree in Urban and Regional Planning from the University of Nairobi, and a Bachelor’s Degree in Engineering from Egerton University. He is also an author whose writings focus on biblical truth, leadership development and the Great Commission. He is married to Mary Wambui Maina.

Source: official biography at cuttingedgeconference.co.ke/about
