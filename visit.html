What to expect

Your first Sunday

Expect heartfelt worship, prayer and a message from the Bible. Our ushers will help you find a seat. There's no pressure to give or to stand up — simply come and be with us.

What time are services?

Sunday service times are being confirmed for this website. Please call the church office on 0722 864 864 before your first visit, or check our Facebook page for the latest announcements.

Where do I park?

Parking details will be added once confirmed by the church office. Our ushers are happy to help when you arrive.

What should I wear?

Come as you are. You'll see everything from Sunday best to casual wear.

Is there something for my children?

Please ask the welcome team on arrival about children's ministry during the service.

Can I watch online first?

Yes. Services and messages are shared on our YouTube channel and Facebook page.
