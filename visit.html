What to expect

Your first Sunday

Expect heartfelt worship, prayer and a message from the Bible. There's no pressure to give or to stand up — simply come and be with us.

Planning to visit? Tell us

What time are services?

Every Sunday: First Service 7:30–9:00 am and Second Service 10:00 am–12 noon (as published on the church's Sunday Services posters). Special services may differ — check Facebook or call 0722 864 864.

Where is the church?

General Kariba Street (Kariba Road), Freehold, Nakuru. Use the map on this page or search “Deliverance Church Nakuru” in Google Maps.

Where do I park?

Parking details will be added once confirmed by the church office. Ushers are happy to help when you arrive.

What should I wear?

Come as you are.

Is there something for my children?

Please ask the welcome team on arrival about children's ministry during the service; details will be published once confirmed.

Can I watch online first?

Yes — both Sunday services are streamed live on YouTube every week.
