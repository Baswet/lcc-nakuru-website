Our purpose

Equipping the saints

The conference takes its theme from Ephesians 4:12 — equipping the saints for the work of the ministry. It serves the LCC congregation and the wider body of Christ in Nakuru and beyond, and is held every April. Held every April since 2018 (not held 2020–2022 due to COVID-19).
