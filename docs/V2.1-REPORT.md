# LCC website v2.1: photography, curation and motion

**Date:** 26 November 2026 · **Branch:** `v2.1-photography-motion` (built on `main` @ 5f75370) · **Status:** deployed to `main` (GitHub Pages): `af6ec9b` (v2.1) and `3a98120` (v2.1.1 image-fallback fix).

## What v2.1 does

v2.1 continues v2; it is not a redesign. The palette, Fraunces + Inter Tight, navigation, content model, admin, forms and all v2 functionality are kept. In line with the latest brief, v2.1 is **more intentional rather than bigger**: fewer images and sections, a better hero, photographs used as design, and restrained motion.

### Other pages
- **About:** cut from 13 to 9 sections. Ministries, Events and Stories were removed because each duplicated another page or had no approved content. The worship and conference rails, the gradient placeholder and the 9-image collage (now 5) were also removed. The pastor placeholder became a typographic medallion.
- **Leadership:** a typographic medallion replaces the "awaiting photo" gradient. **No portrait was added, because none could be verified** (see below).
- **Ministries:** the duplicate image grid became a typographic index. Each ministry has its own frame: offset hairline, arch, circle orbit or pill.
- **Visit:** the 4-step journey uses large numerals (no images), plus one expanding photograph ("Come as you are", desktop only).
- **Events:** heading motion and staggered lists. Expired events never show as upcoming (v2 rule, still in force).
- **Conference:** a layered 2026 collage, labelled **2026** and never 2027.
- **Gallery** (now in the main navigation): editorial asymmetric spread, film strip (drag, swipe, arrows) with a full-screen photo viewer (keyboard, swipe, Esc, focus return), and the existing stream-moments collage, trimmed from about 30 to 12.

## Photography
- **Source:** 65 official LCC YouTube videos × 3 captured frames = 195 frames reviewed. **32 kept** (`content.json → library`): congregational worship, worship teams, Cutting Edge 2026, preaching, the Leaders' Summit, the Pastors' and Spouses Conference, and InterVarsity.
- **Rejected:** posters and title cards, frames with burned-in lyrics, near-duplicates, frames where a child may be the subject, and a recurring intro frame of a couple whose identity is not stated.
- **Captions** state only what the video title says (event and date). No names are guessed.
- **Facebook photographs:** 154 captured at 2048 px (zip in the user's Downloads). **They are not published**, pending written approval from the LCC media team. To add an approved photo: `{"slug":…, "src":"assets/photos/x.jpg", "approved":true, "cat":…, "alt":…}`.
- **Lead Pastor portrait: not verifiable.** cuttingedgeconference.co.ke/speakers lists Rev. Elijah Mwangi with no image. The image beside his biography on /about has no caption or alt text, so it cannot be confirmed to show him. It is **not used**. Captioned official photos exist for 2027 guest speakers Bishop Steve Kamau and Bishop Darnell Williams; they can be added with the conference team's permission.
- **Hosting:** stills are served by YouTube's image service (the same basis as v2). Downloading them locally was not possible from this environment (network allow-list). The fallback chain (maxres → hq → brand background) means a failure never shows a broken icon.

## Motion
Implemented in `assets/js/motion.js` (no dependencies) and `assets/css/enhance.css`:
- Word, character and line split reveals; clip, arch, circle and organic image reveals; scroll-ink text.
- Staggered groups; a single rAF scroll loop; an expanding photograph.
- Slideshow, film strip and lightbox controllers; image fallbacks.

**In production:** headings, image reveals, scroll-ink, staggers and the expanding photo on Visit. **Kept to the lab only** (per "fewer, purposeful" animation): marquee, parallax, pinned story, background scene changes and the extra slideshows. `prefers-reduced-motion` and no-JS were both tested: nothing is hidden.

## Deployment
- Pushed to `main` on 26 Nov 2026: `5f75370 → af6ec9b → 3a98120`. GitHub Pages publishes from `main`.
- **Live check (Chrome, real images):** the v2.1 homepage was live with the new hero, the Gallery nav link and enhance.css. The test found one real defect: in a background or minimised tab (viewport width 0), Chrome aborts image loads, and the hero then stayed hidden. **v2.1.1** makes the images retry when the tab becomes visible; this was verified in an automated test.
- **Rollback:** `git reset --hard 5f75370 && git push --force-with-lease origin main`, or revert the two commits.
