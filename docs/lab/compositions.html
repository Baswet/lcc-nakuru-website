Lab · 01

Photographs as design.

Eight composition techniques using authentic LCC photographs. Each is marked Selected, Use sparingly or Not used.

C1

Arch hero with orbiting circle

Homepage hero. One dominant arch (the sanctuary shape), a circular inset that overlaps it, and a rotating type seal carrying the verified vision. The text column on the left stays unchanged. Selected

Transforming Nations for Christ

A place to celebrate life in Christ.

Life Celebration Centre exists to reconcile people to God and equip them for ministry — right here on General Kariba Street, Nakuru.

Plan your visitWatch a message
C2

Three overlapping circles

Community moments in three sizes, overlapping like a conversation. Faces are kept clear of the overlaps by setting a focus point for each photo. Selected

Life together

Church is a family before it is a building.

After every service the courtyard fills with greetings, catch-ups and prayer.

C3

Ringed portrait

Leadership. A circular portrait inside two hairline rings (clay and line colour). Calm and dignified, with no heavy shadow. Selected

Lead Pastor

Rev. Elijah Mwangi

Leads Life Celebration Centre and convenes the annual Cutting Edge Conference.

C4

Organic masks

Two soft, hand-drawn blob masks for the youth and fellowship pages. Used once per page at most, so it stays special. Use sparingly

Youth Ablaze

Set on fire.

Worship nights, mentorship and bonfires for LCC teens and young adults.

C5

Layered editorial collage

One dominant photograph, a second overlapping from below right, a small detail shot and a sand disc behind. Good for About and the conference recap. Selected

Sunday at LCC · Freehold, Nakuru
C6

Double-line arch frame

Architectural frame for the church building and the 'Plan your visit' page: two hairlines echo a doorway. Selected

Find us

General Kariba Street, Freehold

Ushers meet you at the gate and help you find a seat.

C7

Offset frame

A hairline rectangle offset behind the photo. Quiet and editorial; for ministry and event pages. Selected

Outreach

Into the streets of Nakuru

Open-air ministry brings worship and the gospel to the city centre.

C8

Pill and oval portraits

Tall pill frames for people-led stories (testimonies, ministry leaders). Avoid for groups. Use sparingly
