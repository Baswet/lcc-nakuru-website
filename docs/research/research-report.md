## 4. LCC social and content observations

- **YouTube** is the strongest owned channel: regular Sunday streams ("Sunday Service | Second Service"), sermon clips titled "Message | Speaker", worship covers by One Accord Ministers, and conference sessions. *Implication:* the site should be "watch-first", with a latest-message hero and a sermon library.
- **Facebook** (14K) carries announcements, event posters, devotion posts and live streams. *Implication:* link to it for daily updates, not mirror it.
- The **Cutting Edge Conference** is LCC's biggest external-facing event and has its own site with registration. *Implication:* give it a featured band on Home and its own page, linking to the official registration.
- **Identity cues:** "Transforming Nations for Christ" carries a missional, outward tone, and "Life Celebration" is joyful and celebratory. Nakuru context: Rift Valley, Lake Nakuru, Menengai Crater. These suggest a warm, earthy, celebratory palette rather than corporate blue.

## 5. Limitations

- Instagram needs a login, so it was not viewed.
- Facebook pages block screenshots (the renderer freezes). The text was read instead.
- dcairview.org did not resolve ("This site can't be reached") on 25 Nov 2026.
- Pinterest boards need a login for full browsing, so no Pinterest collections were captured. Mood references were taken from live sites instead.
- cuttingedgeconference.co.ke denied in-page script analysis. A screenshot and the text read earlier were used instead.
- Reference-site targets in the brief (15 international, 10 African, 10 Pinterest, 5 editorial, 5 conference) were not all met. 16 sites were captured; see `competitor-analysis.md`.
