## Patterns that win

1. **"New here?" or "Plan your visit"** as the primary CTA (Highlands, Saddleback, Living Streams).
2. **Watch-first**: latest message or live status high on the page (Life.Church, Transformation, Elevation).
3. **Service times in or near the hero** (DC Umoja, Nairobi Chapel).
4. **Intent-based next steps** (Saddleback): "I want to…" lists convert better than ministry jargon.
5. **Serif display type on warm or dark grounds** reads premium and less corporate (Bethel, Living Streams).
6. **Local identity**: Mavuno's African craft pattern and Living Streams' terracotta set them apart from US templates.

## Gaps LCC can own

- None of the Kenyan DC sites has a fast, image-light, mobile-first experience. Nakuru users are mostly on mobile data. **Target: home under 300 KB before video, with no autoplay video.**
- Most local sites mix poster images with text. LCC will use **real HTML text** for every announcement (accessible and SEO-friendly).
- Conference promotion is split across sites. LCC will give **Cutting Edge a permanent featured band** linking to official registration.
