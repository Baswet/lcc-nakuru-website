## The design language these produce: "Editorial Warmth v2"
1. **Ivory editorial pages** for reading (About story, Visit, Give).
2. **Night cinematic bands** for watching (Worship Experience, Watch Live, Conference universe).
3. **Conference universe** with its own personality: night background, sun-gold, oversized numerals and dates.
4. **Streaming rows** for sermons: horizontal scrolling rows by category (Sunday Services, Praise Express, Conferences, Worship).
5. **Original Kenyan linework** (an SVG band inspired by kitenge and kiondo weaving geometry) as section transitions, used sparingly.
