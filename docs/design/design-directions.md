# Design Directions

Three directions were coded as working pages in `concepts/`. Screenshots are in `concepts/shots/` (1440 and 390 px, JPEG; side-by-side views in `concepts-desktop.jpg` and `concepts-mobile.jpg`).

| | A — Modern Editorial Elegance | B — Cinematic Worship Experience | C — Contemporary Creative Ministry |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mood | Calm, literate, trustworthy | Dramatic, worship-night energy | Joyful, community, youthful |
| Ground | Ivory paper #F6F1E8 | Night #12100E | Sand #F3E7D6 |
| Type | Fraunces (serif display) + Inter Tight | Anton (condensed caps) + Inter Tight | Manrope (geometric sans) |
| Accent | Clay #A8482A | Sun #F3C567 / Ember #E0892F | Clay, ochre, flamingo |
| Strength | Most premium; ages well; reads well in long text | Strong for Watch / live / conference | Intent tiles; warm Kenyan colour; mobile-friendly |
| Risk | Can feel quiet for youth | Dark UI is heavy on data; needs real photography | Can look playful or "app-like" for a 55-year-old denomination |

## Selected: hybrid "Editorial Warmth"
- **Base from A**: ivory paper, Fraunces headlines, hairline rules, generous whitespace. Used for every page body.
- **Bands from B**: dark "night" sections for *Watch & Worship*, *Live*, and the *Cutting Edge Conference* feature. This gives cinematic contrast without making the whole site dark.
- **Tiles from C**: "I want to…" intent tiles and the ochre and flamingo accents for next steps.
- Rejected: Anton (too loud for long use) and fully rounded app chrome.

The design avoids navy, emerald, purple, cyan, neon, glass cards and glowing borders, as the brief requires.
